the battle of stalingrad

i chose the scene at the end of the movie where alan talks about all the battles the allied forces won, and they couldn’t have won without them. and i’m specifically talking about the short clip we see from the battle of stalingrad. The battle of Stalingrad was a great battle and a decisive turning point in world war 2 that happened in the city of Stalingrad in the soviet union. The fight lasted a little over half a year. It started on the 17th of july 1942, and it ended on the 2nd of february 1943 with the German general in charge of the operation surrendering. the battle was between germany (with help from romania, hungary and italy) and the soviet union, and the reason this battle was so important is because it was the main event that stopped germany’s advance into the soviet union and it significantly weakened germany’s forces. hitler and the nazi party chose to attack Stalingrad for 2 main reasons. they wanted to cut off russia's supplies from coming through the river of volga which is in stalingrad, and they also wanted to win the city for propaganda purposes because the city was named after joseph stalin himself. i used https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-Stalingrad and https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/military-history-and-science/battle-stalingrad/ for my sources and they are trustworthy because the stuff written in them was written by people who has spent years studying it. The battle of Stalingrad is only shown for a few seconds as a part of a bigger scene where Alan Turner talks about all the wars the allied forces wouldn't have been able to win because of them. In the scene we also see clips from D-day and the battle of ardenne which are 2 of the most famous and important battles from ww2. The scene has a kinda scary and eerie soundtrack playing in the background while we hear Alan saying the names of the different wars with clips from the wars showing on the screen. the visuals shown are real clips from the wars so I guess they used full lenses and they probably didn’t care to much about how they filmed it considering they were in the middle of a battle. I think they did it this way to make it feel more real. if they played it out with actors it wouldn’t really capture the feeling as well as it did in the movie. the clips from Stalingrad and Ardenne are black white as those were the only types of cameras they had, but the clips from Normandie and Omaha beach are actually in color.
